Google had a busy May. It rolled out its second core update of 2026 on May 21, redesigned the search box around AI at I/O, and made Gemini 3.5 Flash the default model in AI Mode. Reports at the time said AI Mode had reached around 1 billion monthly users after its first year.

## What Google actually says

Google's own guidance on generative search features is refreshingly dull: optimising for AI Overviews and AI Mode is still SEO. Those features draw on the same index and ranking systems, so crawlable, helpful pages are the entry ticket. There is no secret submission form for AI results.

## What changed for searchers

AI Mode queries are about three times longer than classic searches, and a growing share use voice, images or video. People ask full questions now. A page built around one short keyword can miss that, while a page that answers the follow-up questions has a better chance.

## What we would do

- Write for questions, not just keywords. Answer the next question before the reader asks it.
- Show real expertise: who wrote it, what they know, where the facts came from.
- Fix the basics: crawlable pages, fast loading, clean titles and descriptions.
- Add structured data where it fits, such as FAQ and organisation markup.
- Do not over-optimise for AI citations. Some analysts suspect the May update targeted exactly that.

## What this means for your business

Rankings will keep wobbling around updates, so judge SEO over months, not days. We build sites with the technical side done properly from day one, then help with content that answers what your customers actually ask.
