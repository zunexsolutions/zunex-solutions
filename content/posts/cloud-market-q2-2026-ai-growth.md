Synergy Research Group says enterprise spending on cloud infrastructure services reached USD 143 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up more than USD 43 billion from a year earlier. Growth was the highest in eight years, and the market has roughly doubled over the last 11 quarters.

## Who has what

Amazon leads with 28% of the market, Microsoft has 20% and Google 15%. Microsoft and Google are growing faster than Amazon. Synergy also tracks a second tier of newer providers, including CoreWeave, Oracle and Nebius, that are growing quickly. The analyst's own explanation is that generative AI lit a fire under the market: AI-specific cloud services are growing at around 165% a year.

## Why it is not your buying guide

Market share tells you who is big, not who is right for you. For a small business, the questions are different. How predictable is the bill? Does your team know the platform? Do you actually need a hyperscaler, or would a simple managed host and a database do the job?

## Keeping costs sane

- Start small and add services only when you need them.
- Set budget alerts on day one.
- Turn off what you are not using, especially test environments.
- Review your bill monthly. Surprises are cheaper to fix early.
