Shopify's mid-2026 Editions release on June 17 shipped more than 150 updates, and nearly all of them circle one idea: your store should be able to sell inside AI assistants like ChatGPT, Copilot and Google's AI Mode, not only on your own website.

## What shipped

The centre piece is the Universal Commerce Protocol, an open standard built with Google that covers discovery through checkout. Alongside it came Shopify Catalog, public MCP servers and an Agentic Storefronts section in the admin where you can see which AI channels show your products. There is also an Agentic plan for merchants who are not on Shopify. Separately, legacy Shopify Scripts stopped running on June 30, 2026, so check any old checkout customisations.

## What is real, what is early

Discovery is real: shoppers do find and compare products inside assistants. Fully autonomous checkout is shakier. Forrester analysts note the checkout step is hard to replicate in that channel, and OpenAI pulled its Instant Checkout in March 2026. Availability also varies by country, and some regions are still early access.

## How to prepare

- Clean up product data: clear titles, accurate attributes, good images, correct stock.
- Check the new Agentic admin section and confirm your settings.
- Search for your own products in an AI assistant and see what appears.
- Keep your own storefront fast. Customers still land there.
