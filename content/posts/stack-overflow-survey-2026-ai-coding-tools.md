Stack Overflow published its 2026 developer survey this week, and the AI results sum up the moment well: people use these tools constantly, and they do not take the output on faith.

## The numbers

Among respondents who use coding assistants and agents, 73% use them daily. On trust, 48% said they trust AI output when they can easily verify it, while only 6.6% trust it for many tasks including important work decisions. VS Code remains the most used editor by a wide margin. In the assistants category, Claude Code was reported ahead of GitHub Copilot, with Copilot second, used by 58.7% of respondents to that question.

## What the pattern says

The tools have become part of the workflow, like autocomplete, but nobody is handing over the keys. The sensible reading is that AI speeds up drafting while review, testing and design decisions stay human. Teams that skip review tend to meet the bill later as bugs and security holes.

## How we use these tools

- Assistants help with boilerplate, tests and first drafts.
- Every change goes through human review and automated checks.
- Security-sensitive code, such as auth and payments, gets extra scrutiny.
- We tell clients plainly where AI helped.
