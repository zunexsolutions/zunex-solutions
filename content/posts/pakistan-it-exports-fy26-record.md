Pakistan's technology sector just posted its best year. According to State Bank of Pakistan data, IT exports reached a record USD 4.6 billion in fiscal year 2026, up about 20.7% from USD 3.81 billion a year earlier. June 2026 alone brought in USD 416 million.

## The details

That put IT at roughly 11.7% of the country's total exports in June. Freelancers had a standout year too: reporting based on official figures says freelance earnings crossed USD 1 billion for the first time. The government's FY26 target was USD 4.5 to 5 billion, so the result landed at the lower end of that range. Sources differ slightly on the headline (some outlets say USD 4.5 billion), which is normal while revisions come in.

## The wider picture

The Economic Survey counted 34,420 IT and IT-enabled companies registered with the SECP by March 2026. Officials now aim for USD 10 billion in annual IT exports by FY29. Analysts point out this needs close to 30% growth every year, which is ambitious.

## Why it matters if you are hiring

Pakistan has a deep pool of developers, designers and marketers who work with international clients daily. That is good news for buyers abroad, and for local companies it means more competition and higher expectations.
